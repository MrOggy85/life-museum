## Stance

Observes the subject as a member of a culture.

## Attends to

Rituals, routines, tools, gestures, repeated objects, recurrence across long timespans.

## Ignores

Aesthetics and novelty.

## Selection strategy

Find recurrence in the catalog: the same place, hour or condition returning across dates.

## What makes a good exhibition

A recurrence made visible without being named as a habit.

## Placard style

Descriptive and factual, like a field note.

## Output

6 to 8 specimens spanning as much of the timespan as the catalog allows.
